To start off, the website itself is very well put together. It immediately hooks viewers in utilizing very detailed artwork and animations depicting the aftermath of a nuclear blast. The website not only goes very much into depth regarding the risk of nuclear war increasing again due to rising global tensions, but also incorporates a visual aspect of storytelling that many websites don't usually have. It makes the message the author is trying to convey linger in the minds of its viewers as many articles in the NY times don't really take the time to do this. They simply tell the information to the viewer.
